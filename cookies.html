    Cookies Policy

    Last updated: 14 April 2025 · Effective immediately

    Brevora Living Lines (“we”, “us”, “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze site traffic, and understand where our readers come from. This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, which ones we use, and how you can manage them.

    Your control matters: You can accept, refuse, or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect how the site functions, but you’ll still be able to read our content.

    1. What are cookies?

    Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet, phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide anonymous usage data to site owners. Cookies do not contain viruses and cannot access your personal files.

    We also use “pixel tags” (also called web beacons) — tiny graphics that help us understand how you interact with our emails and pages. These work similarly to cookies.

    2. How we use cookies

    We group our cookies into the following categories:

    • Strictly necessary cookies: These are essential for the site to function properly — for example, enabling you to navigate between pages, access secure areas, and use basic features. Without these, the site may not work correctly.
    • Performance & analytics cookies: These collect anonymous information about how visitors use our site — which pages are most popular, how long readers stay, and if they encounter error messages. We use this data to improve our editorial content and site design.
    • Functionality cookies: These remember your choices (such as language or region) to provide a more personalized experience. They may be set by us or by third-party providers whose services we use on our pages.
    • Advertising & targeting cookies: We may allow carefully selected advertising partners to set cookies to deliver relevant ads and measure campaign effectiveness. These cookies do not identify you personally, but they may track your browsing across sites.

    3. Specific cookies we use

    Below is a list of the main cookies currently active on Brevora Living Lines. We may update this list as our site evolves.

    Cookie name Type Purpose Duration
    brevora_session Strictly necessary Maintains your session while you browse the site; ensures basic functions work. Session
    brevora_prefs Functionality Stores your cookie consent preferences and display settings. 1 year
    _ga Analytics (Google) Used to distinguish unique users and track page views anonymously. 2 years
    _gid Analytics (Google) Used to distinguish users for analytics reporting. 24 hours
    ads/ga-audiences Advertising Used by Google Ads to re-engage visitors based on online behavior. Session / up to 1 year
    fr Advertising (Meta) Used by Facebook to deliver targeted advertising and measure conversions. 90 days
    IDE Advertising (Google) Used by Google DoubleClick to record and report user actions after viewing or clicking ads. 1 year

    Note: Third-party cookies (e.g., Google, Meta) are governed by the respective privacy policies of those providers.

    4. Your cookie choices

    When you first visit our site, you may see a cookie consent banner allowing you to accept or manage your preferences. You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Here’s how to manage them in popular browsers:

    • Google Chrome — Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data.
    • Mozilla Firefox — Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
    • Safari — Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data.
    • Microsoft Edge — Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies.

    To opt out of Google Analytics across all websites, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. For advertising cookies, you may visit www.aboutads.info/choices or www.youronlinechoices.eu.

    5. Do Not Track signals

    Some browsers offer a “Do Not Track” (DNT) feature. Our site does not currently respond to DNT signals because there is no industry consensus on how to interpret them. We continue to monitor developments in this area.

    6. Changes to this Cookies Policy

    We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. When we do, we will revise the “last updated” date at the top. We encourage you to check this page periodically to stay informed.
